Privacy Policy

This policy explains how DEDL LTD, a company registered in the Republic of Cyprus under registration number HE 434880 ("DEDL", "we", "us"), handles personal data in connection with the website dedl.eu (the "Site") and when you contact us. It covers all our business lines, including AFFCT Influence Marketing Agency and DEDL Events. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR") and Cyprus Law 125(I)/2018.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is DEDL LTD, Kosta Krystalli 6, Agios Athanasios, 4105 Limassol, Cyprus. For any question about this policy or your data, write to info@dedl.eu.

2. What we collect

Collected automatically. The Site uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, sets no cookies and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. Our hosting provider may log technical data (IP address, browser type, time of request) to keep the Site secure and to deliver it. These logs are not used to identify or profile visitors.

What you give us. When you contact us by email, phone or messenger, we receive what you choose to share: for example your name, contact details, company and details of your project.

Data of influencers, guests and participants. When we run a campaign or an event, we may process data about influencers, guests or participants. This is governed by the agreement for that project.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal: steps before a contract, or our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • To deliver services you ordered: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep accounting, tax and anti-money-laundering records: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To keep the Site secure: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only when needed with: service providers (hosting, email, cloud storage, payment providers, accountants, auditors and legal advisers); influencers, suppliers, venues, performers and crew engaged for your project, only as far as they need it; public authorities and banks where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case we rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission, Standard Contractual Clauses or another safeguard allowed by the GDPR.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 24 months after our last contact.
  • Client and project records: for the project and then as long as Cyprus tax and accounting law requires (generally up to 7 years).
  • Guest and participant data: deleted or anonymised after the project, unless the agreement says otherwise.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent. Write to info@dedl.eu. You may also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
